APR Center

Full APR guide →

The Annual Performance Review — a yearly conversation to celebrate progress, name growth, and plan the next season.

1
Schedule
Leader schedules the APR conversation 2 weeks ahead.
2
Self-reflection
Staff fills the Self-Reflection sheet.
3
Leader review
Leader fills the Manager Review template.
4
Conversation
60–90 min: review the year, hear each other, set 2–3 goals.
5
Plan
Together, create or update the Individual Development Plan.
6
Follow-up
Monthly check-ins through the year.
Leader Guide
  • Read the staff's self-reflection beforehand.
  • Open with care and specific encouragement.
  • Be clear AND kind on areas to grow.
  • Listen more than you speak in the first half.
  • Agree on 2–3 focus areas, not 10.
  • End with prayer or blessing if appropriate.
Staff Self-Reflection
  • What am I most proud of this year?
  • Where did I grow most?
  • Where do I feel stuck?
  • What do I want to learn next?
  • What support would help me?

Training Request

Full guide →

How to request training support — a simple 5-step process.

1
Talk to your Leader
Discuss what you'd like to learn and why it matters for your work.
2
Check Available Options
Look in the Training Directory for matching options.
3
Fill the Form
Use the Training Request Form — include the cost, time and value.
4
Wait for Approval
Your leader and HR will review the request together.
5
Prepare for Training
Once approved, plan handovers and prepare to apply what you learn.
