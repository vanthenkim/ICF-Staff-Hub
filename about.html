About ICF Cambodia

Empowering Cambodia · Helping people become more like Jesus Christ and fearlessly change their world.

Our Purpose · Why We Exist

“As a church, it is our passion for people to become more like Jesus Christ, live fearlessly and have a positive influence on their world.”

Our Values

Excellent

We give our best in every small thing — because the people we serve deserve our best work.

Relevant

We meet people where they are — in their language, their culture, and their reality.

Excited

Joy and energy are core to who we are — we bring them to the work and to each other.

Authentic

Real, honest, transparent. We don't perform — we live what we believe.

Hospitable

Every person — staff, visitor, child, family — is welcomed home.

Generous

We give freely — our time, resources, and love — because we have received generously.

What we do every day.
