Property

Maintaining, securing, and improving ICF Cambodia's campus — from daily cleaning and security to vehicles, maintenance, and electrical services.

Org Chart

What We Do

Campus & Buildings
Maintenance and upkeep of all ICF campus buildings, facilities, and infrastructure — ensuring a safe and functional environment.
Maintenance & Repairs
Handling maintenance requests for electrical, plumbing, carpentry, and general repairs across all buildings.
Security
Round-the-clock security for the campus — managing access control, patrols, and incident response.
Cleaning & Grounds
Daily cleaning of all facilities, plus gardening and groundskeeping to maintain a welcoming campus environment.
Vehicles & Logistics
Managing organisational vehicles — scheduling, maintenance, and driver coordination for staff transport needs.
