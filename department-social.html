Social

We are dedicated to fostering the healthy personal development of children and young people by strengthening their identity, competence, and living environment — empowering them to build strong families and positively influence their communities.

What We Do

Community Outreach
Connecting with vulnerable families and communities in Siem Reap through relational outreach, needs assessment, and holistic support.

Family Care Program
Karano
Comprehensive family support from early childhood to adulthood through regular home visits, parenting education, and crisis intervention.
· Personalized monthly home visits
· School performance monitoring
· Counselling, parenting training & financial literacy
· Health & nutrition support (in partnership with medical team)
· Emergency response — abuse, school dropout, mental health
Daycare Programme
Full-day childcare for children aged 1–5 — serving ICF staff children and Family Care toddlers in a safe, nurturing environment.

Medical Care
Prevention-focused health support across all social teams — hygiene, dental, nutrition, and first aid education, with referrals for serious cases.
· Health education for beneficiaries & ICF staff  ·  Collaboration across all social teams
Mother Care Program
Pregnant women & mothers with infants — birth prep, breastfeeding support, hygiene & nutrition
Mental Health Care
Awareness-raising, basic counseling, and staff support — with counseling specialists embedded in all teams and referrals to external professionals for complex cases.
