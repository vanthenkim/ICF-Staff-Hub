Donor Care

Growing ICF Cambodia's support base through meaningful donor relationships, transparent stewardship, and accountability for every gift entrusted to us.

Org Chart

What We Do

Donor Relations & Care
Building and maintaining meaningful relationships with individual and organisational donors — keeping them connected to ICF Cambodia's mission and impact.
Donor Reporting & Accountability
Producing accurate financial reports and impact updates for donors — ensuring full transparency and accountability for every gift entrusted to us.
Fundraising Strategy
Developing and executing fundraising campaigns, donor acquisition strategies, and long-term support growth for ICF Cambodia’s programmes.
Donor & Supporter Visits
Coordinating and hosting visits from donors, partners, and supporters — creating meaningful, memorable experiences that deepen their connection to ICF Cambodia.
Hospitality & Accommodation
Arranging accommodation, meals, transport, and logistics for visiting guests — ensuring every visitor feels welcomed and well cared for.
Campus Tours & Programme Exposure
Leading guided tours of ICF Cambodia’s campus and programmes — helping guests see firsthand the impact of their support.
Mission Teams
Coordinating and hosting short-term mission teams — welcoming international visitors, organising meaningful service experiences, and connecting teams with ICF Cambodia’s work and impact.
